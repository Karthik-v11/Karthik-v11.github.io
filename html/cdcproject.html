Industrial Logistics Redefined
Logistics on a busy industrial floor can get messy fast. At the Packing station, we needed to close the loop on every single box leaving the factory. I developed the Packing System to tie everything together—from scanning QR codes with Zebra handhelds to printing labels on the fly.

This wasn't just a simple tracking app; it was a full ecosystem involving an ASP.NET 6 API, a Blazor Web Portal, and a Flutter handheld app. I integrated native hardware communication protocols like Zebra DataWedge and ZSDK to ensure that operators could scan and print with zero latency.

The result was a 90% reduction in manual entry errors. By automating the pallet-to-box mapping and providing management with a real-time "Andon" display, we turned a chaotic warehouse floor into a streamlined, data-driven operation. Mastering these industrial hardware integrations was a huge milestone in my engineering growth.
