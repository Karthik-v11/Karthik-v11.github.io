The Challenge & Impact
Quality is the backbone of precision engineering, but paper checklists and complex manual math were slowing things down on the factory floor. I built QSure 360 to bring the entire Quality Assurance process into the digital age.

The ecosystem features Gunaastra for mobile-first field inspections and Geometric Test for high-precision tablet-based testing. I automated the geometrical tolerance calculation engine, ensuring that every measurement is verified instantly against engineering standards. By integrating Firebase Cloud Messaging, I established a real-time feedback loop between inspectors and the assembly team.

This platform secured 100% process compliance and digitized what was previously a fragmented, error-prone workflow. It taught me how to manage deep industrial business logic and robust state management in Flutter, ensuring that critical data is captured accurately even in the high-pressure environment of the shop floor.
